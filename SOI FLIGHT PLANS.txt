PersistentFlight 0.17 experimental - flight plans across SOI changes

Start the idle mission pilot as before. Focus the craft at normal time and
create chronological map nodes, including a node on the incoming patch
of the destination body. Open PF -> Mission actions and choose
Queue flight plan across SOI changes. The normal Queue entire flight plan
button retains its same-body behavior. Both require an idle pilot CPU.

Example workflow: transfer burn around Kerbin, then a capture node on the
planned Mun encounter patch. No transfer/capture nodes are generated for
you. The map solver must already show the relevant future patches.

The SOI plan saves up to 32 nodes and enables the normal recovery boot.
It finishes a burn, keeps the next body-specific node queued, and waits
with engines stopped until the craft reaches that node's reference body.
It never computes a burn by subtracting velocities about different bodies.
After arrival it recomputes the velocity correction against the SAVED
post-burn target orbit; it preserves the planned burn time and objective.
It does not optimize a new trajectory or guarantee an encounter/capture.

Arrival checks require: the expected body, a loaded unpacked craft at 1x,
a usable chemical-engine fuel estimate, enough fuel for the corrected
burn, a target periapsis above atmosphere depth + 1 km (not a terrain scan), position
error at burn time no more than 20 km, correction no more than the larger
of 150% of planned delta-v or planned delta-v + 50 m/s, and at least the
estimated full-throttle burn duration + 60 seconds until the node.
A tiny correction below 0.5 m/s requires manual review too. Existing
reserve forecasts remain advisory; insufficient total fuel holds arrival.

A failed arrival check holds the mission and preserves the waiting node.
Inspect it, cancel the saved plan, and create a revised map plan. Resume
rechecks the same saved target; it cannot repair an incorrect trajectory.
An unexpected intervening body holds the plan when its CPU is loaded.
A sleeping craft must be visited to report that fault. Plans support
consecutive direct body hops between burns, not unrecorded intermediate
body passages such as Kerbin -> Sun -> Duna without a Sun-frame step.
An overdue next burn holds the queue when the CPU loads, instead of
executing it late.
Changing body during an active burn also holds the retained request.

Visit this craft automatically near its arrival burn is enabled by default
for SOI plans. If the craft is unloaded after arriving at the expected
body, PF performs its existing saved native travel to that craft within
180 seconds of the next node. This changes your active vessel and creates
PersistentFlight-Travel.sfs in the save. It lets the kOS recovery boot load
and run the arrival burn. You can turn the option off and visit manually.
The handoff only runs at normal time, with a 15-second retry spacing.
It requires the game to stay in a flight scene. Sleeping CPUs are not
executed on another celestial body by an independent distant live region.

Coast warp leases now stop short of the next waiting node and an upcoming
native SOI patch transition. Automatic travel does not advance time.
Use the existing guarded warp controls; a packed CPU cannot renew its
lease. The feature does not provide unattended high-warp travel from the
tracking station or Space Center. Visit early for slow CPU/script startup.

SOI status and automatic-visit preference persist with the CPU, along with
its queue and recovery settings. Cancellation clears the entire saved
plan. A waiting plan reserves its craft against a distant analytic burn.
Close KSP before installing. Back up your complete save first. Do not
open future-body queue saves with an older mod; restore a matching old
save if downgrading. The four-field node format remains readable, but old
versions do not understand the waiting/handoff rules.

kOS commands:
 ADDONS:PFR:QUEUESOIPLAN(): capture a future-body plan from map nodes.
 ADDONS:PFR:SOIPLAN: whether an SOI plan is active.
 ADDONS:PFR:SOISTATE: current SOI plan status.
 ADDONS:PFR:SOITAKEOVER: read/write automatic arrival visit preference.
 ADDONS:PFR:COASTUNTIL: current safe coast lease deadline.
 QUEUECOUNT includes waiting nodes; NODEQUEUED is false while waiting.
 CANCELNODE clears waiting plans as well as active burns.

An event mission can send QUEUE_SOI_PLAN to the idle included pilot.
Queue must be captured while that craft is focused at normal time.
Timeline events: SOI_PLAN_WAITING, SOI_PLAN_READY, SOI_PLAN_HELD,
SOI_PLAN_COMPLETED. Type these names into the event editor.

Fuel panel: a plan containing future-body nodes shows a nominal budget
using current vacuum engines. It must be rechecked after arrival;
the arrival controller separately validates its corrected burn cost.
Check map plan remains a same-body preflight check.

Validation scope is in SOI-VALIDATION.json. The native new-feature test
uses a natural Mun escape into Kerbin, real burns in both frames, a cold
waiting-plan reload, an unloaded coast while observing another craft,
and automatic native visit for the second burn. A Kerbin-to-Mun capture
mission is not directly flown by that fixture. Safety thresholds, invalid
arrival inputs and timing boundaries are checked independently.
