PersistentFlight 0.22 experimental — landing reports

Approaches descending below 10 km above local terrain are observed for
craft with a ModuleRegionalAutonomy and landing reports enabled. Toggle
Record approach and touchdown report in Mission actions. No landing
controller is added: your guidance still flies the craft and stock KSP
provides ground/water contact. Landing takeover remains available.

Flight debrief now includes landing reports alongside burn reports:
* Ground/water contact location and selected-target surface distance.
* Last observed speed before stock contact, with vertical/horizontal speed.
* Remaining LiquidFuel, Oxidizer and ElectricCharge, plus lost part count.
* Settled touchdown, part loss, destroyed craft, or interrupted observation.
The CSV contains these measurements and target-availability explanations.

Contact is confirmed by native KSP LandedOrSplashed and observed for two
seconds to include immediate part loss. Resource/location values reflect
the settling window; speed is the last pre-contact sample, not a collision
impulse measurement. Lost parts compares original part IDs with survivors;
it is not an assessment of every possible mod's damage system. A destroyed
recorder or vessel can leave a partial report. Dead-vessel telemetry does
not replace the last live speed/resource measurements. Unknown resource
values show Unavailable and export as empty CSV cells. Reports never
change thrust.

Saved open approaches close as Interrupted: save reloaded during approach.
Packed/unloaded craft, time warp and scene changes close partial observation.
Bouncing or leaving before settling is reported as interrupted. A later
observed descent may create another approach report. Already landed craft
do not create a report merely because a save was loaded. Missing/wrong-body
surface targets show unavailable distance; no substitute target is invented.
Target distance is great-circle surface distance, not deck collision error.

Save format 2 loads previous burn reports from format 1. New landing fields
are persisted with strict validation. Unsupported/damaged history retains
the existing recording hold and evidence preservation behavior. Landings
share the existing 1,000-report history and whole-game recovery branches.

LANDING-VALIDATION.json records the actual tests and their limits. Ground
and water tests use native falling craft/contact, without setting a landed
flag. Hard-impact tests use native collisions. Carrier-deck landings,
precision guidance, magnetic capture and all third-party damage models are
outside this release's test coverage.
