Interpretation and next work:
The update removes repeated fleet membership searches and per-step physics buffers.
Monitoring/telemetry improved; live-physics frame times did not show a consistent gain.
The loops-disabled diagnostic measures remaining game/other-mod cost, not a pure stock install.
Keep monitored and fully physical ship counts separate. Large live missions with active kOS,
atmosphere, terrain contact and complex engines still need their own capacity tests.
Use the native measurements to design capacity reservations; no automatic admission policy
or Lodestone gameplay bridge is implemented by this release.

Real native KSP vessels. 15-second uncapped windowed 1280x720 observations after warm-up; 180-second sustained monitoring case. Monitored cases include one future queued burn per remote vessel; burn cases make the named subset due. Telemetry snapshots are enabled, CSV recording is disabled. Other installed mods and occasional stock asteroid spawns remain active. kOS CPUs inactive for capacity cases; powered live craft use harness-set 5% throttle. Analytic jobs use a real loaded-vessel engine capture mapped to identically constructed remote vessels; initial remote dispatch API capture is not tested by this fixture. Core/regional tick summaries are last-256-sample windows, not the entire burn interval; diagnostic peak core windows are sampled every half second. Memory/GC counts include whole process, measurement lists and forced telemetry capture. Single observations per case/build; not a universal capacity guarantee.
