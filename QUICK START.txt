PersistentFlight 0.25 experimental

Focus an idle orbital craft at normal time.
Open PF -> Mission templates and enter a name.
Select Circular orbit at altitude and enter the target altitude in km.
Save orbital goal, then select it to preview the calculated plan.
Scroll to see both burns, the transfer/final orbit and total fuel estimate.
Calculate fresh goal creates the native map plan.
Inspect both map nodes, then Mission actions -> Queue entire flight plan.
A target already at an existing apsis needs only one circularization burn.
Preview and map creation do not start thrust.
Previous maneuver sequences and the four original goals remain available.
