PersistentFlight 0.25 experimental

Adds a reusable goal to reach a circular orbit at a chosen altitude.
Calculates an apsis transfer followed by circularization at arrival.
For intermediate targets, compares the supported apsis transfers by
estimated total delta-v; preserves the orbital plane.
Reduces an existing target apsis to a single circularization burn.
Previews all burns, transfer/final orbit and combined fuel advisory.
Retains explicit map review and queueing, recovery and normal flight reports.
Rejects unsafe or unqueueable plans and known body changes before completion.
Existing PFMT1 sequences and the four PFMT2 goal types remain compatible.
See CIRCULAR ORBITS.txt and CIRCULAR-VALIDATION.json for exact scope.
