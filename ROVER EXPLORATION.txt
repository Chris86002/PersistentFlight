PersistentFlight 0.15 experimental: rover exploration

Create a saved route of 1 to 64 latitude/longitude stops. The stock-wheel
rover slows for terrain and approaching stops, brakes at each waypoint,
and records travel points about every 20 metres for a retraced return.
Routes run in existing native/regional physics at normal time. Existing
same-body live range and capacity requirements still apply. This release
does not simulate rover driving while packed or on a distant body.

STARTING A ROUTE
1. Focus a settled surface rover with stock motorized wheels and an idle
   kOS CPU. Finish/cancel any maneuver queue or analytic burn first.
2. Open PF -> Rover routes. Select its CPU.
3. Enter stops in decimal latitude/longitude. "Add 100 m east" builds a
   simple straight route from the rover or last draft stop.
4. Choose cruise speed (0.25-5 m/s), arrival radius (2-20 m), slope limit
   (2-30 degrees), reserve (5-80%) and stuck timeout (15-600 game seconds).
5. Save the route. It starts paused. Home is the current rover position.
   The CPU boot selection changes to boot/pf_rover_route_resume.ks; the
   previous selection is restored when you clear the finished route.
6. On that CPU, run:
       RUNPATH("0:/pf_rover_route.ks").
7. Choose Resume route. After checking motion, focus another craft on
   the same body. The existing automatic regional physics handles driving.

PAUSE, RECOVERY AND RETURN
Pause stops wheel commands and applies brakes. Resume clears the route's
latched terrain/stuck/power hold, then immediately checks current conditions
again. A separate fleet mission hold must also be reviewed and released.
Return home retraces the recorded path, including intermediate points.
It does not clear a hazard hold. You can return after completing all stops.
Cancel closes the route; Clear restores the previous boot selection.

Full game saves retain waypoints, progress, recorded path, energy estimate,
return cursor and pause/hold. Recovery boot runs the included driver after
a normal CPU reboot. It preserves a pause or hazard hold. Loading rebases
the movement watchdog so time spent unloading does not count as being stuck.
Invalid checkpoint data is preserved and held, including craft ownership;
restore a complete save or explicitly discard the damaged route in the UI.

TERRAIN AND POWER CHECKS
The controller samples surface heights ahead and across a three-metre
corridor, over an 8-25 m look-ahead. Excess slope, unavailable terrain or
ocean crossings stop the route for player review. This checks terrain
heights, not every rock, building or collision obstacle. No automatic
detour planner is included. Keep initial tests slow on open terrain.

Battery reserve initiates automatic return if enabled; otherwise it holds.
After measuring net battery drain over 10 m, the rover also estimates the
energy needed to retrace the route, using a 50% margin and 5% capacity
allowance. This estimate depends on the observed craft and terrain; it
cannot guarantee sufficient energy on steeper return legs. At 1% charge,
the rover holds even during return. Solar generation can reduce measured
net drain. Critical power and stuck detection still apply.

No progress of at least 0.5 m toward the current target within the chosen
timeout causes a latched stuck hold. Pauses and unsupported wheels do not
consume this timeout. The 1,024-point return trace holds before overflowing.
The driver keeps brakes applied and denies warp while running, including
after arrival. Stop the program explicitly before handing its CPU to
other guidance. Clear route state separately through the editor.

MISSION TIMELINE / SCRIPT SUFFIXES
WAYPOINT_REACHED: one event per completed stop, after speed falls below
0.1 m/s within the arrival radius. ARRIVED: final outbound stop complete.
ROVER_RETURNING, ROVER_HOME, ROVER_STUCK, ROVER_TERRAIN_HOLD,
ROVER_POWER_HOLD and ROVER_TRACE_HOLD can trigger event mission rules.
Route save/resume/cancel and status changes are also logged.

PFR:ROUTEID      saved route ID, or empty when absent/damaged
PFR:ROUTEACTIVE  outbound or returning (also true during a route hold)
PFR:ROVERRUN()   sample safety/progress and update the saved route
PFR:ROUTESPEED   current requested speed, zero while paused/held
PFR:ROUTETARGET  latitude/longitude target for wheel steering
PFR:ROUTESTATE   readable route status
PFR:ROUTEINDEX   number of outbound stops reached
PFR:ROUTESLOPE   last sampled maximum slope, degrees

Use the provided driver or a cooperating script that honors all holds
and zero-speed requests. The API does not prevent unrelated scripts from
commanding wheel controls on another CPU. The route editor rejects a
known maneuver pilot/queue and another saved route on the same vessel.
